7 Bonus: 20-minute filler — Would You Rather?

No prep needed. Read one prompt. Students choose a side (stand left or right, or hands up), then call on 2–3 to explain why. Great for any grade 4–8 class.
  1. Would you rather have a pet dragon or a pet dinosaur?
  2. Would you rather be able to fly or be invisible?
  3. Would you rather do homework for a month or clean your room for a week?
  4. Would you rather visit the past or the future?
  5. Would you rather have unlimited pizza or unlimited books?
  6. Would you rather be the smartest person in the room or the funniest?
